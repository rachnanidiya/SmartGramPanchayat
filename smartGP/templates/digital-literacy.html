Digital Literacy program

About the Program

The Digital Literacy Program aims to empower villagers by equipping them with essential digital skills. This initiative helps bridge the digital divide, improve employment opportunities, and enable access to government services online.

Key Features

  • Basic computer literacy (MS Office, typing, internet browsing)
  • Online safety and cybersecurity awareness
  • Access to e-learning platforms and resources
  • Introduction to digital banking and online transactions
  • Training on using government e-services (Aadhar, PAN, etc.)

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a resident of a rural or semi-urban area
  • Minimum age: 15 years (no upper limit)
  • Basic reading and writing skills required
  • No prior digital knowledge needed

Resources & Learning Material

  • Digital India Initiative
  • Microsoft Digital Literacy Curriculum
  • Khan Academy - Free Online Courses
  • Government-certified online training modules (Available in regional languages)

Benefits of the Program

  • Increased job opportunities in digital sectors
  • Better access to online education
  • Secure online transactions and digital banking knowledge
  • Improved digital awareness and safety
